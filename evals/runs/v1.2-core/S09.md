TCP is connection-oriented and reliable: it sets up a connection first, checks that every packet arrives, resends lost ones, and delivers data in order. That reliability costs some speed and latency. It suits web pages, email and file transfers.

UDP is connectionless and best-effort: it sends independent datagrams with no handshake, no delivery check and no ordering guarantee. It has less overhead and lower latency, but lost or out-of-order packets stay lost. It suits DNS, video and voice calls, and online games.

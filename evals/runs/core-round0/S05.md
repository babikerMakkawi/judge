## skill_en / opus
### t1
**Go with MediDesk (C)**, as long as you can pay the €6,000 onboarding once, from reserves, and keep it out of your €500/month.

**Why**
- **Cheapest over two years:** €10,800 in total, against €11,400 for CareFlow. After that it's €275/month cheaper.
- **Room to hire:** users are unlimited. CareFlow hits exactly €500 with one more hire and goes over with two.
- **Patient data stays in Germany.** CareFlow keeps patient records on US servers. That's a serious data-protection risk, and you'd need legal sign-off before using it.

PraxisPro is out at the quoted price: €480 + 2 × €30 = €540/month.

**Main risk:** you'd commit two years and about €10,800 to a product you haven't used. CareFlow's 30-day exit and good reviews are what you give up. Before signing:
- Have your front desk and two therapists run a typical week in a demo account: recurring appointments, prescriptions, treatment notes. Also call a physio practice that uses it. If it's clumsy, walk away.
- Get these in writing:
  - what the €6,000 covers
  - a data processing agreement (AVV)
  - a C5 security certificate (German rules, § 393 SGB V, generally require one for cloud-hosted health data if you treat GKV patients)
  - full export of your data when you leave
  - the price after month 24

**Check VAT:** if these prices don't include VAT, add 19%. Physio practices usually can't reclaim it because treatments are VAT-exempt (your Steuerberater can confirm). CareFlow would then cost about €565/month, over your limit. MediDesk would be about €238/month plus about €7,140 for onboarding.

**If the €6,000 can't come from reserves,** none of the quotes fits cleanly. Try these in order:
1. Ask MediDesk to spread the fee over the contract. That makes it €450/month, but over €500 if VAT is added.
2. Ask PraxisPro to cover all 12 logins for €500 or less.
3. Use CareFlow only if it can store your data in the EU, passes the same checks and stays under €500 with VAT.

1. If paying €6,000 upfront is a problem, ask MediDesk to spread the fee over the contract. That makes it €450/month, which is under your limit.

If MediDesk won't spread the fee and you can't pay it upfront, ask CareFlow whether they can keep your data in the EU. AWS has a data centre in Frankfurt. If they can, go with CareFlow.
